Interchanges worth knowing

  • KL Sentral — the hub: KJL, Monorail (short walk to KL Sentral Monorail), KTM, and MRT Muzium Negara via a linked walkway.
  • Masjid Jamek — KJL ↔ AGL/SPL cross-platform, the busiest interchange in the old city core.
  • Pasar Seni — KJL ↔ MRT Kajang Line, plus a bridge to KTM Kuala Lumpur station.
  • Putra Heights — southern loop where KJL and SPL meet.
  • Kwasa Damansara — where both MRT lines begin; cross-platform between KGL and PYL.

Which technology am I riding?

It matters less than it sounds, but: MRT trains are the newest and longest; LRT is driverless on the Kelana Jaya line; the Monorail is the short two-car line threading through Bukit Bintang. All use the same Touch 'n Go / tokens / MyRapid cards.
