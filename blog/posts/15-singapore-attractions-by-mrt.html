Singapore's MRT stops at, or right underneath, almost every major sight. The detail that saves time is the exit letter — big stations have half a dozen. Station codes like CC34 are on every sign and map, so we've included them.

New in 2026: the Circle Line became a full loop on 12 July 2026, with new stations at Keppel, Cantonment and Prince Edward Road. HarbourFront (for Sentosa) to Marina Bay is now a single train ride, and Bayfront and Marina Bay have new Circle Line codes (CC34 and CC33).

Sentosa & Universal Studios

Start at HarbourFront (NE1/CC29), which opens into VivoCity mall. Then pick one:

  • Sentosa Express monorail from VivoCity Level 3 — S$4 to enter the island, then free rides around Sentosa and back out. Tap a card at the gate. Get off at Resorts World for Universal Studios.
  • Walk the Sentosa Boardwalk from VivoCity (Lobby F, Level 1) — a short, free walk across the water.
  • Bus 123 — the normal bus fare, no entry charge.

Buses and beach trams on Sentosa itself are free.

Zoo, Night Safari & Bird Paradise (Mandai)

Take the North–South Line to Khatib (NS14), leave by Exit A, and board the M2 Mandai Khatib shuttle bus — S$2.50, every 15 minutes, about 20 minutes, cards and phones accepted. Alternatives: bus 138 from Ang Mo Kio (NS16) or Springleaf (TE4), or bus 927 from Choa Chu Kang (NS4).

The airport & Jewel

Changi Airport station (CG2) is on the East–West Line's airport branch — change at Tanah Merah. Jewel is 5–10 minutes' walk over the link bridges from Terminals 2 and 3. More in our airports guide.
