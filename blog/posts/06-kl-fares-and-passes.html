Fares in the Klang Valley are cheap and distance-based, but the passes are where people overpay or underpay. Here's the plain version. Exact sen-by-sen prices change, so treat the figures below as the shape of it and confirm the latest on the operators' own sites.

The one rule that saves you money

Pay cashless (Touch 'n Go / contactless), never cash. Cash single-journey tokens are priced higher than the same trip tapped with a card on every Rapid KL line — the card fare is the real fare.

Passes at a glance

MY50 · MALAYSIANS
RM50/month
Unlimited Rapid KL rail + bus (LRT, MRT, Monorail, BRT, buses). Needs a MyKad. The best deal if you commute more than ~10 return trips a month.
RAPID BULANAN · EVERYONE
RM150/month
Same unlimited Rapid KL network, no citizenship requirement — the option for non-Malaysians and anyone without a MyKad.
1-DAY UNLIMITED
Day pass
A single-day unlimited-travel pass for tourists or heavy one-off days out — worth it once you're making 4+ trips in a day.

Is a monthly pass worth it?

Simple test: a typical rail commute across town is around RM3–5 each way. That's roughly RM6–10 a day, so a full month of weekday commuting is far more than RM50. If you're a Malaysian commuting daily, My50 pays for itself in about a week and everything after is free. Occasional riders should just tap-and-go cashless — no pass needed.

Concessions

  • Students, senior citizens (60+), and OKU (disabled) get concession fares on Rapid KL and KTM with the right registered card.
  • Children below a height/age threshold generally travel free with a paying adult.

KTM Komuter & the airport

KTM Komuter has its own distance-based fares (not covered by the Rapid KL passes) and its own monthly options. The airport trains (KLIA Ekspres / Transit) are a separate premium service — see our KLIA & Changi guide for the cheaper bus and Komuter alternatives.
