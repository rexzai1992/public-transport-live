1. What this service is

Public Transport Live is an independent app showing public transport information for Malaysia and Singapore. It is built on open data from official public APIs, including Malaysia's Open API (data.gov.my / Prasarana GTFS feeds), Singapore's LTA DataMall, OpenStreetMap and CARTO for maps, community flight-tracking networks (adsb.lol, adsb.fi, adsbdb), and Klang Valley line status from the MTREC community API (mtrec.name.my).

We are not affiliated with, or endorsed by, Prasarana, KTMB, LTA, SMRT, SBS Transit, or any transport operator or government agency.

2. All times are estimates

Every time shown in this app — arrival times, wait times, journey durations, timetables, flight ETAs — is an estimate, not a guarantee. Live positions can lag or drop out; timetables change; buses run early or late; upstream data sources can be wrong or unavailable. Do not rely on this app for time-critical travel (exams, flights, interviews, last trains). Always leave a margin and check official operator sources when it matters.

5. Privacy
